WHEN SOMETHING GETS IN THE WAY

Let’s get your
words moving again.

Try the checks below first. They cover the most common reasons a dictation does not start or reach your text field.

Recording will not start

Check Microphone and Accessibility in macOS Privacy & Security, then reopen Talkless if macOS asks. In Dictation settings, verify the selected microphone is connected. Try System default or another available input. If a device disconnects or its route changes, start a new recording.

The model is not ready

Open Settings → Speech recognition. Let the download finish verifying and preparing, or retry a failed download. If your network cannot reach the selected source, choose another supported mirror. For Apple recognition, check the selected language and its speech assets.

Text went missing or landed elsewhere

Keep the intended text field focused until processing finishes. Check History for the completed result and copy it again. Some apps restrict pasting or exposing selected text. Try a normal text field in Notes to narrow down whether the issue belongs to one app.

AI is unavailable

Check your provider, key and model in AI service settings. Configuration complete is not proof of a successful connection. Test Connection sends a fixed sample and may incur a provider charge. A polishing failure keeps the original transcript; translation and voice editing show their own errors. Never share your API key in a bug report.

Report a reproducible problem

Use the GitHub repository link in the header, then choose Issues. Include your Talkless version and build, macOS version, Mac model, speech engine, microphone and clear steps to reproduce. Say what you expected and what actually happened. This website does not collect or submit reports.

If diagnostics would help, use Settings → System → Diagnostics to export a local ZIP. Review its contents before attaching it; optional crash reports may include personal information and file paths. Nothing is automatically uploaded. Avoid sharing private dictation or selected text.
