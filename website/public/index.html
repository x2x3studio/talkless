Local speech stays local.

With local Qwen recognition, audio is processed on your Mac, not uploaded. Download the speech model separately, then dictate offline.

↗

Cloud is a choice, not a catch.

Optional cloud recognition sends audio to the speech provider you configure. Optional AI sends the text needed for polishing, editing, or translation to your own AI provider.

◎

Your history lives with you.

Dictation history and diagnostic logs stay on your Mac. Logs are bounded to 15 MB and exclude audio, transcripts, API keys, and prompts. Nothing is automatically uploaded.

⌘

An app you can look inside.

Talkless is source available and distributed directly on GitHub. The app does not collect telemetry. This website has no analytics or tracking scripts; its host may keep standard request logs.

BEFORE YOU SAY HELLO

A few good questions.

Will it work on my Mac?

Talkless requires an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later. Intel Macs and older macOS versions aren't supported. The current release is a beta, so expect a few rough edges.

What does getting started look like?

Download the DMG, drag Talkless into Applications, and open it. The setup guide walks you through Microphone and Accessibility permissions. Then download the local speech model in Settings → Speech recognition. AI setup is entirely optional.

Do I need an account or a subscription?

No Talkless account is required. Local recognition runs on your Mac after the model download, with no hosted Talkless service. If you choose cloud speech or AI features, bring your own provider credentials and check that provider's pricing and privacy policy.

Does it replace my keyboard or input method?

Neither. Talkless lives in the menu bar and inserts text into the field you're using. Keep that field focused until the result arrives. Some apps restrict text insertion or access to selected text; the user guide explains the controls and limitations.

Can I use it in English and Chinese?

The app interface supports English and Simplified Chinese. Recognition language support depends on your selected speech engine and model. Optional AI translation and output language settings are separate from the interface language.

Where do I find help or report a bug?

Start with the user guide for setup and shortcuts. Our support page covers common problems and what to include when reporting a bug. User guide → Get help →
