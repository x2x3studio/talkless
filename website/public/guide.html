A SMALL GUIDE TO GETTING STARTED

From a thought
to a text field.

Start with local dictation. Add cloud speech or AI only if you want them. No Talkless account is needed.

Download for Mac

Apple Silicon · macOS 26+ · Source available

Install and prepare

Use an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later. Download the beta, open the DMG, and drag Talkless into Applications. Open that copy of the app.

Follow the setup guide to allow Microphone and Accessibility access. Microphone records your voice; Accessibility enables shortcuts and text insertion. Skipping setup does not grant these permissions.

In Settings → Speech recognition, download the local Qwen3 ASR · 0.6B model (about 713 MB). Wait for verification and preparation to finish. Model weights are separate from the app and are not hosted by Talkless.

Speak and finish

Click a text field, hold Fn, speak, and release. Tap-to-toggle mode is also available in Settings → Dictation. Pausing your speech does not end a recording. Escape cancels recording or processing.

Keep the intended text field focused until the result arrives. Talkless pastes at the current cursor using the clipboard, then restores the previous clipboard unless you copied something else. Recover finished text from History if needed.

Fn is the default, not a requirement. Set the dictation key and AI polishing shortcut in Dictation settings. If Fn opens the emoji panel, change the Globe-key action to Do Nothing in macOS Keyboard settings, or use another key.

Choose your speech engine

Local Qwen and Apple on-device recognition can work offline after their required models are available. They provide live text. Cloud Whisper-compatible recognition sends the full recording to your configured provider after you finish, and may incur charges.

Choose System default or a specific microphone in Settings → Dictation. A disconnected selected microphone is not silently replaced. If the input route changes during a recording, check the device and start again.

Use AI only when you want it

Configure your own provider, credentials and model in Settings → AI service. Polishing is off by default. During recording, the AI polishing shortcut (Right Option by default) finishes this input and does the opposite of your saved polishing default: Use AI polishing or Skip AI polishing. It does not change that default.

Voice editing is separately enabled in Dictation settings: select text and speak an editing instruction. Translation uses Control-Option-T by default: select text, review the result, then Copy or Replace Original. These features send the needed text to your AI provider. Review output before relying on it.

Keep useful words and results

Add preferred spellings in Settings → Dictionary, and optionally the spellings each term is often heard as; those exact spellings are replaced after recognition. Blue dictionary marks mean a confirmed spelling is present, not that a correction is guaranteed. Review suggestions come from local comparisons of raw and polished history; only terms you accept join your dictionary, and Always fix also adds the heard spelling as a fix. Import and export use plain text.

History keeps up to 500 recent results on your Mac. Search, copy or delete entries there, or use Copy Last Result in the menu bar menu. Clearing history and clearing daily activity are separate actions. Interface language, recognition language and AI output language are separate settings too.
