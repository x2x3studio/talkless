A SMALL GUIDE TO GETTING STARTED

From a thought
to a text field.

Start with local dictation. Add cloud speech or AI only if you want them. No Talkless account is needed.

Download for Mac

Apple Silicon · macOS 26+ · Source available

Install and prepare

Use an Apple Silicon Mac running macOS 26 or later. Download Talkless, open the DMG, and drag Talkless into Applications. Open that copy of the app.

The setup guide starts by confirming your microphone and its permission, then lets you customize the dictation key and hold or tap mode. Accessibility enables global shortcuts and text insertion; skipping setup does not grant permissions.

In Settings → Voice input, choose Offline and download the local Qwen3 ASR · 0.6B model (about 713 MB). Wait for verification and preparation to finish. Model weights are separate from the app and are not hosted by Talkless. App updates do not require downloading the model again.

Speak and finish

Click a text field, hold your dictation key, speak, and release. In tap mode, press once to start and again to finish. Configure both in Settings → Voice input or Setup Guide. A speaking pause does not stop recording; Escape cancels.

Keep the intended text field focused until the result arrives. Talkless uses a queued clipboard paste, not an input method. It keeps the text available for a short compatibility interval before restoring your previous clipboard, unless you copied something else. Posting paste keys does not prove the destination accepted the text. Finished results remain in History.

Fn is the default, not a requirement. Change the key in Voice input settings. If Fn opens the emoji panel, set the Globe-key action to Do Nothing in macOS Keyboard settings, or choose another key. Settings opens from the menu bar, without a Command-comma shortcut.

Choose your speech engine

In Settings → Voice input, choose Offline or Doubao online. Offline uses the local model; previews periodically reprocess accumulated audio and the final pass covers the complete recording, with safe internal windows for long audio. Doubao uses your Speech API key and ASR 2.0 resource. Auto keeps live recognition; a specific input language uses the supported streaming-input endpoint and may return text later. Spoken cleanup is a separate Doubao option, not AI polishing. The menu bar offers engine and online-language switches. Optional offline fallback requires a downloaded model.

The microphone is first in Voice input settings. System default shows the actual device; it means the default audio input, not direct capture of your Mac's playback. A specific missing device is not silently replaced. Capture failures or a three-second gap in audio callbacks cancel quietly; ordinary silent buffers do not. Start a new dictation after checking the device.

Doubao online means Volcengine’s Doubao Speech ASR service. Obtain a Speech API key in the Volcengine console, not a Doubao app login or an Ark chat-model key. It is an optional third-party service; the official terms are linked on our privacy page.

Use AI only when you want it

Settings → AI polishing contains its switch, provider, credentials, model and writing preferences. It is off by default and separate from recognition. Polishing requests have a five-second deadline; timeout quietly uses the recognized text. Settings → Text formatting controls local punctuation and spacing without an AI service.

Voice editing is enabled on the AI polishing page: select text, hold the dictation key with Control (Option if your key is Right Control), and speak an edit. The dictation key alone types. Translation defaults to Control-Option-T and has its own language settings. Review output before relying on it.

Optional experiments

Settings → Labs contains Enhanced recognition and Sentence alternatives, both off by default. Enhanced recognition sends the same audio to local Qwen and Doubao, then gives the available transcripts to your AI service. It requires the local model, Doubao credentials and an AI service; one failed route stops while the other continues. It can use more resources and take longer; improved accuracy is not guaranteed.

Sentence alternatives appears only when AI returns two or three different full versions. Changed words are highlighted. Click a whole sentence; hover to pause the countdown and leave to resume. Timeout uses the marked default. The wait defaults to five seconds and can be set from two to fifteen. Explicit same-language choices can feed dictionary learning; timeout is not user approval.

Keep useful words and results

Settings → Dictionary shows each entry as heard → written. Talkless learns on its own: when AI polish keeps making the same spelling fix, that fix takes effect automatically, and it retires after 30 days without use, for example once recognition gets the word right. Learned words also become recognition hints, so mistakes get rarer over time. Add your own entries too; they never retire. Everything stays on your Mac. Blue dictionary marks mean the spelling appears in the text, not that a correction is guaranteed. Your own entries can be imported and exported as plain text.

History keeps up to 500 recent results on your Mac. Search, copy or delete entries there, or use Copy Last Result in the menu bar menu. Clearing history and clearing daily activity are separate actions. Interface language, recognition language and AI output language are separate settings too.
